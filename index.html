Feature Engineering Methodology

SalesCast Pro engineers 12 distinct time-series features across three critical dimensions:
• Lag Signals: Yesterday’s revenue (Lag 1) and exact day last week (Lag 7) capturing immediate auto-correlation.
• Momentum Envelopes: 7-day and 30-day moving averages alongside volatility dispersion (rolling std).
• Calendar Seasonality: Day of week, weekend flags, week of year, day of month, and multi-year trend time.
